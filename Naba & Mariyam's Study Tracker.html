Who’s using this?
Each block is a week. On a personal tab the fill shows that person’s ticks, and on Together it shows tasks you have both finished. A pink block means one of you is busy or away that week. Set your own weeks on your page.

Rules for the paper and talk
  • Research Paper: no generative AI at all. Spell and grammar checkers are fine, but recheck every quote and reference afterwards.
  • Lightning Talk: AI is allowed only for visuals built from your own ideas. Evaluate and edit anything it makes, and include an acknowledgement line and reference.
  • Keep 3,600 to 4,400 words for the paper, take your names off every file, and submit through Turnitin with Harvard references.
  • Talk: aim for 5 minutes (7 maximum), recorded, then fill in the Google form. Expect viva questions on your method and results.

Naba’s and Mariyam’s pages are separate, and only the owner can change their own. Both of you see each other’s progress on the Together tab. Your changes sync when the page is shared with edit access, and are always kept on this device.
